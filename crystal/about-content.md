## SECTION 4 — Our Journey / Heritage (timeline cards, B&W product imagery)
### The Beginning In 1971
"Crystal's origin traces back to a man who challenged the status quo — one that accepted household chores as tedious, time-consuming, and complex. Tools developed through scientific innovation abroad were available in India through the grey market, but remained out of reach for the majority and were not always dependable. This led to the birth of Crystal's core idea: creating tools for everyday household chores that are durable, easy to use, and above all, affordable."

### The Piezoelectric Gas Lighter
"In those days, kitchenware products were positioned in the premium segment and were largely imported. The Chairman's vision was to offer Indian customers international-quality products manufactured in India at Indian prices. The journey began with flintstone lighters, evolved to piezo lighter, and gradually expanded to a wide range of kitchen products and home solutions — all with the aim of simplifying the daily chores of Indian homemakers."
- [IMAGE NEEDED: gas lighters + stove, B&W]

### The Surgical Steel Kitchen Knife
"Kitchen knives were once sold in India as a commodity, lacking both durability and precision. Foreign-made alternatives available through the grey market were also beyond the reach of the common man. Crystal recognized this opportunity and, through its R&D, developed blades made from surgical steel that retained their sharpness for a longer time."
